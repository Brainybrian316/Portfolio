How do I aim and throw?

Slide on the square Aim pad below the field. Lift to keep your aim, then tap Throw on the right. Buttons on the left switches to precise direction and reach controls. Help also offers optional classic drag-and-release controls.

Pick up the parcel on the outward arc, reach the mailbox on the return, and avoid hedges. Free levels preview your current aim; Expert Adventure levels show direction and reach without the full flight path.

How do stars and retries work?

A first-throw delivery earns three stars. Two or three throws earn two; four or more earn one. Retries are unlimited. Replay starts a fresh run, and your best earned stars are kept.

Where is my progress saved?

Stars, route selection, cosmetic choices and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or automatic cross-device progress sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings.

Deleting the app may remove local progress. Restoring a pack restores access, not level stars from another device.

What about optional packs?

The planned public game includes 74 free levels. The Style Pack adds cosmetic designs and trails with the same flight and scoring. Adventure adds 50 expert levels. Optional packs are planned as one-time purchases, without ads, subscriptions or waiting timers.

The game is not publicly released and real purchases are not currently available. When enabled, purchases and purchase restoration will be handled through Apple. If pack access is missing after release, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase, or email support. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Can I adjust accessibility settings?

The game supports separate aim buttons, system text sizes, Reduce Motion and optional haptics. You can turn haptics off in Help. If a control is difficult to use, tell us which device, text-size setting and screen are affected.
