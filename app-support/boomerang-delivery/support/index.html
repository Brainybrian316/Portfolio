How do I aim and throw?

Slide on the square Aim pad below the field. Lift to keep your aim, then tap Throw on the right. Buttons on the left switches to precise direction and reach controls. Help also offers optional classic drag-and-release controls.

Plan the outward and return arcs, collect parcels and reach the mailbox. In version 2.0, free levels introduce mechanics with a full flight guide, then mix full, half and no-guide challenges. Expert Adventure levels use half guides or no guide. Magnets can help shape a route; black holes are dangerous, so watch their moving or changing fields.

How do stars and retries work?

In version 2.0, each level has its own throw target. Finishing at or below that target earns three stars; using up to two additional throws earns two; taking more earns one. Check the target shown in the game. Retries are unlimited. Replay starts a fresh run, and your best earned stars are kept.

Where is my progress saved?

Stars, route selection, cosmetic choices and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or automatic cross-device progress sync. Device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings.

Deleting the app may remove local progress. Restoring a pack restores access, not level stars from another device.

What about optional packs?

Version 2.0 includes 174 free levels and an optional Adventure Pack with 100 Expert challenges. Complete the earlier levels in each campaign to unlock new ones. Previously completed levels remain available to replay; Adventure Pack access is still required for its levels. Existing Adventure Pack owners receive the expanded pack without buying it again. The Style Pack adds cosmetic designs and trails without changing flight or scoring. Packs are optional one-time purchases, without ads, subscriptions or waiting timers.

Apple handles purchases and purchase restoration. If pack access is missing, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase, or email support. Apple handles billing and refund requests. The App Store shows current pricing for your region.

Can I adjust accessibility settings?

The game supports separate aim buttons, system text sizes, Reduce Motion and optional haptics. You can turn haptics off in Help. If a control is difficult to use, tell us which device, text-size setting and screen are affected.

How does the early-adopter gift work in version 2.0?

The free early-adopter skin and trail are for qualifying players who first acquired the eligible original version from the App Store before the version 2.0 release cutoff. The app checks verified Apple information on your device against a small public release policy. A missing connection or pending release policy can delay verification, but it does not block gameplay. Previously granted gifts remain available offline.

The gift notice is shown once on the current app installation, not every launch. Deleting app data or moving to a new device can reset that local notice marker. If eligibility cannot be verified, use the gift-check option in Help or contact support. No receipt or Apple Account details are sent to this support website.
