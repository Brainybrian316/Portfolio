Privacy

How Boomerang Delivery and its app-support pages handle information. Other apps and the rest of the portfolio may have different practices.

Last updated: 26 October 2026. Boomerang Delivery is not publicly released.

The game and on-device progress

Boomerang Delivery, by brainybrian316, is an offline puzzle game and does not require a game account. Stars, route selection, cosmetic preferences and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. There is no automatic cross-device progress sync. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

The reviewed app contains no advertising or analytics service and no developer-operated gameplay server. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not send gameplay data to the creator.

Apple purchases

The game is not publicly released and real purchases are not currently available. When optional purchases are enabled, Apple handles payment and account processing under its own policies. The app processes verified transaction information to provide and restore pack access. It does not receive payment-card details.

Restoring a pack does not restore level progress from another device. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

Support emails

If you email support, brainybrian316 receives your email address, message, and any attachments or device details you choose to include. This information is used to respond and investigate the issue. The email provider also processes and stores correspondence as part of delivering the service.

A support email is not anonymous. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card information or unrelated sensitive data. Contact the support address with privacy questions or requests concerning your correspondence.

This website

These static app-support pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, embedded third-party widgets, forms or browser-storage features. Email links open your chosen email application; no message is sent until you send it.

The portfolio uses GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of technical request information.

Your choices

You can disable haptics in the game’s Help and use system Reduce Motion settings. Deleting the app may remove locally saved progress. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple purchase.
